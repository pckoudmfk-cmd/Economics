How the rotation works

  1. Your whole team shares one live workspace: any teammate can type an answer on their own device, and it appears for everyone on the team within a second or two.
  2. Each station opens with a short brief (objective and context) explaining which GFP department has requested the work.
  3. The teacher starts the 8-minute timer at the top of the page for the current station.
  4. Where a task has one correct answer (classification, matching, gap-fill), any teammate can press Check answers for instant feedback — this locks the task for the whole team at once.
  5. Open tasks (memos, case analysis) are reviewed by the teacher and stay editable throughout.
  6. The teacher's dashboard shows every team's progress live, so results don't need to be collected by hand.
  7. On the last station, press Download results to save your team's complete set of answers as one file.

Draft report — business structure analysis

A sole corporation is the simplest form of business. It is easy to establish and gives the owner shared control over all decisions. However, the owner also carries limited liability for all business debts and financial obligations, which means personal assets may be at risk.

A limited liability company (LLP) is more complex. It combines flexibility in taxation with limited liability for its members. This structure is popular among small and medium-sized enterprises that want to raise capital without losing operational control.

A corporation can raise capital through the issuance of bonds. This allows the company to access a large number of investors. However, corporations often face rigid taxation issues and must comply with complex regulatory requirements and maintain a clear governance structure.
